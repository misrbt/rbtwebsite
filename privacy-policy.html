Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

1. Introduction

RBT Bank Inc., A Rural Bank ("RBT Bank," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its implementing rules and regulations, and the applicable circulars of the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have over it.

This policy covers information collected through this website, our branches, and our customer service channels. It does not cover the practices of third-party websites we may link to.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact information you provide when opening an account, applying for a loan, or submitting our Contact form — such as your name, address, phone number, email address, and government-issued ID details.
  • Financial information relevant to deposit accounts, loan applications, and other products, such as income, employment details, and transaction history.
  • Website usage information, such as pages visited and general device/browser information, collected automatically to help us maintain and improve the site.
  • Communications you send us, including messages through our Contact form or the on-site chat assistant.

We do not collect account passwords, PINs, one-time passcodes, or card numbers through this website — RBT Bank Inc. does not currently offer online or mobile banking, and will never ask for this information through the site's contact form or chat assistant.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process account opening, loan applications, and other product requests;
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our Contact form or chat assistant;
  • To comply with "Know Your Customer" (KYC), anti-money laundering, and other regulatory obligations under BSP and Anti-Money Laundering Council (AMLC) rules;
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of this website; and
  • To communicate updates about our products, services, or branch network, where you have consented to receive them.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only:

  • With the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, the Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation (PDIC), the AMLC, courts, and other government authorities, where required by law or regulation;
  • With credit information bureaus, as required under the Credit Information System Act;
  • With service providers who help us operate our business (e.g., IT and hosting providers), under confidentiality obligations; and
  • With your consent, in any other circumstance.

5. Data Security

We apply organizational, physical, and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold, in line with BSP information security expectations and the Data Privacy Act. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we continually work to improve our safeguards.

6. Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Be informed that your personal information will be, is being, or was processed;
  • Access your personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated personal information;
  • Object to certain processing of your personal information;
  • Request erasure or blocking of your personal information under certain conditions; and
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, please reach our Data Protection Officer through any of the channels listed on our Contact page.

7. Cookies & Website Analytics

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site, so we can improve it. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some site functionality.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by BSP regulations and other applicable laws, after which it is securely disposed of.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors through it, except where a parent or guardian is opening a product on a minor's behalf through our branches.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please visit our Contact page for current phone, email, and branch details.
