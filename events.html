Event Highlights

RBT BANK @ 60 in the service to the community

RBT Bank had just celebrated its golden year anniversary last March 12, 2026. A series of activities were set to commemorate the bank's founding anniversary to boost its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR).

Caring for the Environment: Mangrove Planting

Recognizing the vital role of environmental stewardship, RBT Bank actively supports mangrove planting activities in the coastal areas of Guiwanon and Tagbocboc, Talisayan. Mangroves serve as natural barriers against storm surges, prevent coastal erosion, and provide a habitat for marine life. By engaging employees and community members in these activities, the Bank helps raise environmental awareness while contributing to the long-term protection and sustainability of coastal ecosystems.

Bringing Health and Hope to Communities

One of the Bank's flagship CSR initiatives is the Free Medical and Dental Clinic conducted in its areas of operation. In partnership with Dingcong Sanitarium Hospital and 4K MBA, RBT Bank brings a dedicated medical team directly to the community. This program provides free medical check-ups, consultations, and essential medicines. It benefits families with limited access to healthcare, helping detect health concerns early and promoting overall wellness.

Branch Lite – Kibawe Opening

RBT Bank expanded its reach with the opening of its BLU Kibawe on January 19, 2025, located along Sayre Highway in Poblacion, West Kibawe, Bukidnon. This new branch brings accessible and reliable banking services closer to the people, strengthening the bank's mission to support local economic growth and provide financial solutions.

Branch Lite – Claveria Opening

The Claveria BLU branch officially opened on October 3, 2025, in Poblacion, Claveria, Misamis Oriental, marking another milestone for RBT Bank. This branch offers services such as salary loans, SME loans, agricultural loans, and savings deposits, supporting financial inclusion and community development.

Timeline of Events

RBT Bank recognizes that effective leadership and dedicated service are strengthened not only from the hard work, but also through meaningful experiences that create growth, appreciation, and renewed commitment. As part of its continuous efforts to value and motivate its people, the Bank has provided opportunities for its officers and employees to travel and experience other cultures beyond the country.

  • RBT BANK INC. 2015 · Singapore

    The Bank's first overseas incentive trip took officers and employees to Gardens by the Bay and Universal Studios Singapore.

  • RBT BANK INC. 2017 · Hong Kong

    A second incentive trip brought the team to Victoria Harbour and the city's landmark attractions.

  • RBT BANK INC. 2019 · South Korea

    Employees explored traditional Korean villages and cultural sites, broadening their perspective beyond the country.

  • RBT BANK INC. 2023 · Japan

    After the pandemic pause, the Bank resumed its overseas trips with a milestone journey to Japan.

  • RBT BANK INC. 2023 · Bohol

    A local getaway to Bohol gave the team time to recharge and celebrate the year's milestones together.

  • RBT BANK INC. 2024 · Da Nang

    The team travelled to Sun World Ba Na Hills in Da Nang, another reward for a year of dedicated service.

  • RBT BANK INC. 2025 · Account Officers

    The Bank's account officers were recognised with a dedicated trip for their work out in the field.
