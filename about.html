About Us

RBT Bank (formerly Rural Bank of Talisayan) was established on March 10, 1966 with a modest capital of ₱200,000 from the Beltran, Alcid and Seriña family led by its founder the late Col. Alejandro G. Beltran Sr. of Talisayan, Misamis Oriental.

Our History

A Legacy of Service and Growth

From our humble beginnings to where we are today, our journey has always been guided by our mission to serve and empower.

Sustaining Stage (1966–1975)

The main concern was on sustaining the operations with only 2.7 million assets and reaching out to the target communities. The founders braved all unfavorable conditions just to bring the good news to the needy people.

Survival Stage (1976–1985)

This decade marked the years of our country's history. With Martial Law in enforcement and with an economy that was not promising, the bank steadily continued to be resilient bringing in more assets to 6.6 million.

Innovative Stage (1986–1995)

The main concern was on sustaining the operations with only 2.7 million assets and reaching out to the target communities. The founders braved all unfavorable conditions just to bring the good news to the needy people.

Expansion Stage (1996–2005)

This decade marked the years of our country's history. With Martial Law in enforcement and with an economy that was not promising, the bank steadily continued to be resilient bringing in more assets to 6.6 million.

Quantum Leap Stage (2006–2015)

The era of modern management using the information technology, opened the gates to global market thereby creating a borderless economy. The issue of bigness and diversification is our greatest challenge of professional management and proper utilization of its resources.

Our Mission

RBT BANK is committed to provide excellent services to its clients, giving a fair return to its investors, enhancing the quality and welfare of its employees and a partner in the socio-economic development of the community towards a brighter future.

Our Vision

A strong and leading bank that creates a difference by adding value to its stakeholders and the communities it serves — enabling every Filipino to achieve financial stability and growth.

Core Values

The Principles that Guide Us

Our core values shape our culture and define how we serve our clients, communities, and each other every day.

God Fearing

God centered; an institution that respects and reveres God in all its decisions and actions.

Excellence

Giving the best in everything we do — better than before, better than others and better than expected.

Accountability

Being responsible to one's actions and decisions at all levels of the organization.

Commitment

Dedication towards work; ability to sacrifice personal comfort for the common good.

Integrity

Having a sense of honesty and truthfulness; upholding trustworthiness in every transaction.

Thrift

Cost effectiveness; living within our means; efficient utilization of the company's resources.

Professionalism

Culture of people who take pride in their work; espouse productivity, transparency and ethical practices.

Teamwork

Unity of purpose; working together as one to achieve success and serve our clients.

Creativity

Ability to think outside of the box; innovating to delight our customers with new solutions.

Responsiveness

Prompt response and awareness to the needs of clients in the ever-changing environment.
